Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Grid detection and digit recognition run on your phone, and normal scans keep only the digits, not the photo. The only time an image leaves the phone is if you choose to send us a camera bug report from your own email app.

Can it read handwritten digits?

Yes. It reads printed puzzle digits and digits written in by hand, and marks them differently so you can see which ones were part of the original puzzle. Check the result in Edit mode before you start.

What if the camera doesn't find the grid?

Make sure the whole grid is in frame and evenly lit. If it still isn't found, switch to manual mode and drag the four corners onto the grid yourself.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, solving and hints work without an internet connection. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Does it just give me the answer?

Only if you ask. Smart Hint explains one logical step at a time and names the technique, so you can learn it. You can also reveal a single cell or the full solution.

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and adds unlimited puzzle saves, auto-correct of conflicting handwritten entries, the full colour theme editor, Auto-fill All Strategies and faster camera processing.
